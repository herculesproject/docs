# Hércules SGI indicators

The functional analysis of the Hercules SGI (CRIS) has a section for Business Intelligence - Indicators that has been used to extract the SGI metrics and align them within the Snowball metrics and framework.

The sections of the Hércules Indicators analysis are (in Spanish):

* Indicadores de transferencia de conocimiento e innovación (SICTI). SICTI stands for [Spanish System for Science, Technology and Innovation](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/portal/site/MICINN/menuitem.7eeac5cd345b4f34f09dfd1001432ea0/?vgnextoid=700e94273d0d4610VgnVCM1000001d04140aRCRD).

+ REP-BI-0010- Patentes
+ REP-BI-0020-EBTs
+ REP-BI-0030-Contratos
+ REP-BI-040- Oferta tecnológica

* Indicadores CRUE. CRUE stands for [Conference of Principals of Spanish Universities](https://www.crue.org/).

+ REP-BI-0050- RRHH
+ REP-BI-0060- Ingresos
+ REP-BI-0070- Proyectos y contratos
+ REP-BI-0080- Startup
+ REP-BI-0090- Sexenios
+ REP-BI-00100- Investigadores
+ REP-BI-00110- PRC

* SIIU (Recursos humanos). SIIU stands for [Integrated University Information System](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/portal/site/MICINN/menuitem.8ce192e94ba842bea3bc811001432ea0/?vgnextoid=9fca7a95d789b610VgnVCM1000001d04140aRCRD&lang_choosen=en). The analysis section holds indicators from the Human Resource area.

+ REP-BI-0130- Matriculación

* OTRI. OTRI stands for Technology Transfer Office from the University of Murcia.
* UGI. UGI stands for Research Management Unit from the University of Murcia.
